# FOLEY & SAMPLE SOUND DESIGN REFERENCE GUIDE

**Project:** Project 35: Willy Nelson (9/11 Master Mixtape Rendition)  
**Sample Format:** 24-bit / 48 kHz PCM WAV (Stereo)  
**Target Headroom:** -6.0 dBFS True Peak  

---

## 🎧 Asset Catalog & DAW Placement

### 1. `PS5_Startup_SubDrop.wav` (Track 05: A.Rob)
* **Location:** Bar 01 (0:00) — Intro
* **Sound Profile:** High-frequency crystalline console chime followed by a clean sub-bass sweep down to 35 Hz.
* **Mixing Cue:** High-pass filter the chime above 400 Hz; allow the sub-drop to bypass the high-pass filter at 0.8s with a 30Hz brickwall safety cut.

### 2. `Car_Trunk_Slam_Footsteps.wav` (Track 05: A.Rob)
* **Location:** Bar 29 (1:15) — Outro
* **Sound Profile:** Gravel parking lot footsteps followed by an acoustic vehicle trunk latch closure.
* **Mixing Cue:** Pan footsteps slightly left (30% L); center the trunk slam at 0% with 2 dB boost at 60 Hz for physical weight.

### 3. `Methane_Gas_Valve_Hiss.wav` (Track 06: Token)
* **Location:** Bar 01 (0:00) & recurring as ambient riser
* **Sound Profile:** High-pressure pneumatic gas release with rising industrial alarm tone.
* **Mixing Cue:** Notch out 2.5 kHz by -2 dB to prevent clash with fast lead vocal transients; automate stereo width from 50% to 100%.

### 4. `Subsonic_Implosion_Detonation.wav` (Transition: Track 06 ➔ 07)
* **Location:** Bar 57 (1:29) — Outro transition
* **Sound Profile:** Vacuum suction (rising pitch) ending in a clipped sub-impact and hollow reverb tail.
* **Mixing Cue:** Mute all drum stems on downbeat of 1.0s; let the sub-impact tail ring out into complete silence before Track 07 Rhodes enters.

### 5. `Subterranean_Rain_Decay.wav` (Tracks 04 & 07: Clejan & Mac Lethal)
* **Location:** Continuous background bed (Tracks 04 outro & Track 07 intro/outro)
* **Sound Profile:** Cold asphalt rainfall with low distant thunder modulation.
* **Mixing Cue:** Level at -18 dB relative to lead vocal; low-pass filter at 8 kHz to keep the high-end open for hi-hats.

### 6. `Hardwood_Sneaker_Squeaks_Arena.wav` (Track 08: MJ)
* **Location:** Bar 01 (0:00) & Verse breaks
* **Sound Profile:** High-traction sneaker friction squeaks on polished court with distant arena crowd presence.
* **Mixing Cue:** Add short slapback delay (80ms) to simulate stadium reflections; dynamic sidechain to snare hits.

### 7. `Referee_Whistle_Echo.wav` (Transition: Track 08 ➔ 09)
* **Location:** Bar 57 (1:47) — Outro transition
* **Sound Profile:** Crisp dual-tone pea whistle with ping-pong stereo echo (L ➔ R).
* **Mixing Cue:** Automated ping-pong delay feedback throw echoing across the stereo field into Track 09's kalimba downbeat.

### 8. `Golf_Club_Titanium_Crack.wav` (Transition: Track 09 ➔ 10)
* **Location:** Bar 57 (1:36) — Track 09 ending
* **Sound Profile:** High-velocity titanium driver impact on a golf ball.
* **Mixing Cue:** Zero tail; instantaneous cutoff at 0.08s into complete silence before Track 10's gramophone strings enter.

### 9. `Twin_Jet_Turbine_Sweep.wav` (Transition: Track 10 ➔ 11)
* **Location:** Bar 57 (1:45) — Track 10 ending
* **Sound Profile:** Jet engines spooling to supersonic thrust with upward frequency sweep.
* **Mixing Cue:** Automate high-pass filter sweeping from 20 Hz to 18 kHz; cut abruptly at 3.5s on downbeat of Track 11's horn blast.

### 10. `Tire_Squeal_Parallel_Parking.wav` (Track 11: Armani White)
* **Location:** Bar 01 (0:00) & Bar 17 (0:36)
* **Sound Profile:** High-friction rubber skid panning across the stereo image, stopping sharply.
* **Mixing Cue:** Automate pan from 90% Left to 0% Center over 1.8 seconds.

### 11. `Tape_Stop_Deceleration.wav` (Transition: Track 11 ➔ 12)
* **Location:** Bar 29 (1:03) — Track 11 outro
* **Sound Profile:** Mechanical analog tape deck brake engaging, slowing audio pitch to a halt.
* **Mixing Cue:** Apply across the entire master instrumental bus of Track 11; crossfade into Track 12 ocean surf.

### 12. `Caribbean_Ocean_Surf_Dub_Siren.wav` (Track 12: Outro: Jamaica)
* **Location:** Bar 01 (0:00) to Bar 29 (1:28) — Ambient bed & dub accents
* **Sound Profile:** Binaural ocean waves with analog dub siren sweeps and Space Echo feedback.
* **Mixing Cue:** Wide stereo image (120% stereo width); spring reverb send (Roland RE-201 emulation) with 45ms pre-delay.
