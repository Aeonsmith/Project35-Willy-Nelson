## 3. Risk Management & Mitigation Register

1. **Risk: Gapless Transition Playback Errors on Certain DSPs**
   * *Impact:* High (Interrupts narrative flow between acts)
   * *Mitigation:* Deliver both segmented gapless master tracks and an official single-file 42-minute Deluxe Continuous Mix edition to all streaming and archive platforms.

2. **Risk: Live Session Musician Scheduling Conflicts (Violin & Horns)**
   * *Impact:* Medium (Potential delay in Phase 2 tracking)
   * *Mitigation:* Lock studio bookings and session player contracts during Phase 1 (Pre-Production).

3. **Risk: Sibilance and High-Frequency Distortion on Saturated Horns/Vocals**
   * *Impact:* Low (Mix harshness at high volumes)
   * *Mitigation:* Strict adherence to split-band de-essing at 6.2 kHz and Pultec air calibration outlined in Technical Appendix.
