# GLOBAL MARKETING & PROMOTION CAMPAIGN ROADMAP

**Project Title:** Project 35: Willy Nelson (9/11 Master Mixtape Rendition)  
**Campaign Lead:** Marketing Lead / Creative Director  
**Target Duration:** 20-Week Lifecycle (T-8 Weeks to T+12 Weeks)

## 🗓️ 5-Stage Milestone Timeline

```
T-8w ──────────► T-4w ──────────► T-2w ──────────► DAY 0 ──────────► T+2w to T+4w
Teaser Phase     Lead Single      Satirical Drop   Global Launch     Dub Epilogue
(Underworld)     (Armani White)   (Don't Play)     (42m Visualizer)  (BTS Breakdowns)
```

### Stage 1: Subterranean Teaser Campaign (T-8 Weeks to T-6 Weeks)
* **Visual Drops:** 15-second ambient clips featuring reverse violin loops from *Clejan 11*, heavy 808 sub-drones, and cryptic mythological runes of Hela & Double G.
* **Encrypted Portal:** Launch of the interactive web portal broadcasting coordinates that trace the underground line from 9/11 origins to Port Antonio, Jamaica.

### Stage 2: Lead Single — "Armani white (winning my future)" (T-4 Weeks)
* **Single Release:** Track 11 drops on all streaming platforms.
* **Music Video:** High-velocity cinematic video focusing on rapid precision driving, street racing aesthetics, and the `{parallel parking}` motif.
* **Short-Form Push:** TikTok/Reels sound bite campaign around the infectious hook (*"Winning my future / Starving the facts"*).

### Stage 3: Satirical Second Single — "(Don’t play golf)" (T-2 Weeks)
* **Single Release:** Track 10 drops with an absurd, satirical parody video of country club elitism featuring oversized golf carts, lawnmowers, and twin jet turbines.
* **Cultural Cross-Promotion:** Outreach to British hip-hop parody and alternative culture creators (*Unknown P / Tim Hawkins* audience alignment).

### Stage 4: Global Mixtape Release & 42-Minute Visualizer (Day 0 / Week 16)
* **Global DSP Release:** All 12 tracks release simultaneously worldwide at 12:00 AM EST with gapless crossfade flags enabled.
* **Continuous Visualizer Stream:** YouTube premiere of the full 42-minute animated visualizer charting the 4-act journey from the subterranean underworld to the shores of Jamaica.
* **Editorial Playlisting:** Active placement on Spotify (*Most Necessary, Alternative Hip-Hop, New Music Friday*), Apple Music (*The Plug, Hip-Hop Spatial Audio*), and Tidal (*Rising*).

### Stage 5: Post-Release Immersion & Dub Epilogue (T+2 Weeks to T+4 Weeks)
* **Jamaica Dub Sessions:** Release of an extended live dub cut of *Outro: Jamaica* featuring live horns and analog Roland Space Echo manipulations.
* **Studio Production Featurette:** Behind-the-scenes engineering documentary breaking down the violin mic placement, Moog bass synthesis, and master LUFS calibration.
