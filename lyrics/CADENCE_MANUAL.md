## 🎙️ Performance Principles Across Acts

* **Act I: The Descent & The Mirror (Tracks 01–04)**
  * *Delivery Style:* Low center of gravity, heavy chest resonance, deliberate half-time pocketing.
  * *Syllable Density:* 6–9 syllables per bar (spacious, cold, heavy emphasis on consonant stops).
  * *Vocal Processing:* Dry center lead with -12 semitone pitched undertone blended at -14 dB (Hela / Double G persona).

* **Act II: Street Level & Methane Sparks (Tracks 05–07)**
  * *Delivery Style:* Aggressive, rapid, syncopated boom-bap into double-time burst cadences.
  * *Syllable Density:* 14–20 syllables per bar on *Token*, dropping to 7–10 intimate syllables on *Mac Lethal*.
  * *Vocal Processing:* High-saturation preamp drive, tight compression (Distressor 4:1), telephone-filtered ad-lib layers.

* **Act III: Archetypes & Absurdities (Tracks 08–10)**
  * *Delivery Style:* Anthemic projection, rhythmic stabs, theatrical accent shifting (*Don't Play Golf*).
  * *Syllable Density:* 10–14 syllables per bar with dynamic pitch jumps and wide gang chorus chanting.
  * *Vocal Processing:* Haas stereo widening on hooks, plate reverb throws, short slapback stadium reflections.

* **Act IV: Ascent & Manifestation (Tracks 11–12)**
  * *Delivery Style:* Ultra-bounce, playful kinetic flow (*Armani White*) transitioning to warm, relaxed spoken-word roots delivery (*Jamaica*).
  * *Syllable Density:* 11–16 syllables per bar on Track 11, settling into 5–8 meditative syllables on Track 12.
  * *Vocal Processing:* High-gloss air shelf (+3 dB at 12 kHz), Opto-leveling (LA-2A), vintage space echo delay tails.
