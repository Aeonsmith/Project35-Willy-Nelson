# Project 35: Willy Nelson (9/11 Master Mixtape Rendition)

A 42-minute conceptual audio narrative and mixtape project bridging subterranean underworld mythology (guided by **Hela** and **Double G**) with street-level hustle, athletic supremacy, satire, and spiritual liberation in **Jamaica**.

---

## 📖 Project Structure & Narrative Acts

* **Act I: The Descent & The Mirror (Tracks 01–04)**
  * *Tracks:* Resentful, Savage Ga$p, Chris Brown (King), Clejan 11 (mirror)
  * *Theme:* Confronting moral debt, ego deconstruction, and classical trap-violin reflections.
* **Act II: Street Level & Methane Sparks (Tracks 05–07)**
  * *Tracks:* A.Rob (PS5), Token (general) {methane gas}, Mac Lethal (heartbreak days)
  * *Theme:* Urban survival, high-pressure combustible atmospheres, and emotional vulnerability.
* **Act III: Archetypes & Absurdities (Tracks 08–10)**
  * *Tracks:* MJ, Messi, (Don’t play golf) {got two golf planes}
  * *Theme:* Athletic mastery colliding with satirical luxury and country-club subversion.
* **Act IV: Ascent & Manifestation (Tracks 11–12)**
  * *Tracks:* Armani white (winning my future) {parallel parking} <starving the facts>, Outro: Jamaica
  * *Theme:* Kinetic precision driving, forward momentum, and sun-drenched one-drop dub resolution.

## 🛠️ Contributor & Production Guidelines

### 1. DAW & Audio File Deliverables
* **Sampling Rate & Bit Depth:** 24-bit / 48 kHz WAV files for all tracking and stem exports.
* **Naming Conventions:** `[Track#]_[TrackTitle]_[StemType]_[Instrument]_[BPM].wav`  
  * *Example:* `11_ArmaniWhite_Stem_SlapBass_104BPM.wav`
* **Tempo & Key Alignment:** Strictly follow the BPM and key grids defined in the production bible.
* **Zero-Cross Headroom:** Leave -6 dB True Peak headroom on all pre-mix stem exports.

### 2. Gapless Transition Engineering
* Inter-act transitions (04 ➔ 05, 07 ➔ 08, 10 ➔ 11, and 11 ➔ 12) require cross-track stem overlap.
* Do not apply hard fades at stem endpoints without consulting the mixing engineer.

### 3. Git & Version Control Workflow
* Submit all lyric drafts, stem manifest sheets, and metadata updates via feature branches.
* Tag commit messages with corresponding task IDs from `PROJECT_35_TIMELINE.csv` (e.g., `[REC-01] Add live violin multi-tracks`).
* Include co-author attribution on all commits:
  ```text
  Co-Authored-By: Warp <agent@warp.dev>
  ```
